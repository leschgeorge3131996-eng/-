# Nested Exception Policy

Default rule: all project reports must be submitted within 10 business days after project close.

Exception A: clinical studies receive 15 business days.

Exception B: clinical studies involving imported devices receive 20 business days.

Exception C: if the imported-device study also involves a cross-border data transfer, the report must be submitted within 25 business days and reviewed by the data compliance office.

Exception C overrides A and B when all conditions apply.

The policy does not create any exception for marketing surveys.
