# Helios Lab Governance Manual

Status: final release bundle for the 2026-2027 pilot. Draft material is retained for audit only.

## Draft section from 2025, obsolete

The draft said guest researchers should request access by 2026-04-10 and that uploads were limited to 25 MB. It also allowed interns to approve temporary access. This section is not current.

## Final rule, effective 2026-09-01

Guest researchers must request access by 2026-10-05 at 18:00 Beijing time. The final upload limit is 64 MB per file.

Temporary access may be approved only by a principal investigator or the compliance office. Interns cannot approve access.

For confidential datasets, offline review is limited to the genomics team and the robotics team. The education analytics team is excluded from offline review.

The appendix example uses 2026-11-20, 80 MB, and an education analytics screenshot. These are examples only.
