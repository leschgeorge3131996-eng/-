# Cross-Version Reimbursement Regulation

## Policy 2025

The meal reimbursement cap for domestic workshops was 120 yuan per person per day. The hotel cap was 800 yuan per night.

## Policy 2026

The meal reimbursement cap is 150 yuan per person per day. The hotel cap is 1200 yuan per night.

## Errata 2026-03

For Shenzhen workshops only, the hotel cap is 1050 yuan per night. This errata overrides the 2026 policy for Shenzhen.

## FAQ

The FAQ says taxi receipts must include pickup time and route. It does not mention a meal-cap exception.

## Meeting notes with unresolved conflict

One note says after-hours taxi approval belongs to Operations. Another note says Finance owns after-hours taxi approval. The bundle provides no priority rule for this conflict.
