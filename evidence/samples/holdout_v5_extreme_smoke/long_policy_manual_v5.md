# Orion Research Access Manual - Mixed Draft and Final Notes

Document status: final package for the 2026 pilot. Some obsolete draft notes are intentionally retained for audit traceability.

## Draft note from 2025, not current

The old draft said that external collaborators should submit access forms by 2026-03-01. It also listed a temporary upload limit of 20 MB. This draft is superseded by the final policy below.

## Final policy, effective 2026-06-15

External collaborators must submit access forms by 2026-07-12 at 17:00 Shanghai time. The final upload limit is 48 MB per attachment.

The offline review room is open only to the medical-imaging group and the robotics group. The education group is not part of the offline review room pilot.

## Appendix example, not a rule

Example screenshots mention 2026-08-01 and a 60 MB sample file. These values are examples only and must not be used as policy.
