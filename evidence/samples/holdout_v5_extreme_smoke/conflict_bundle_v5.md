# Travel Reimbursement Conflict Bundle

## Policy 2025

The 2025 travel policy set the hotel reimbursement cap at 800 yuan per night for domestic workshops.

## Policy 2026

The 2026 policy raised the cap to 1200 yuan per night for domestic workshops. It said the finance team would publish a correction note if a city exception was required.

## Errata 2026-02

Correction note: for domestic workshops in Hangzhou, the hotel reimbursement cap is 1100 yuan per night. This errata overrides the 2026 policy when the destination is Hangzhou.
